Built for any deck

An iPhone and a hardware keyboard, held sideways: that is a deck. A 3D-printed shell, a stand behind a mechanical board or a folding travel keyboard, over Bluetooth or a cable.

What DeckTerm does

  • Keyboard first

    Your servers on one screen, each with its system’s logo. ⌘1–⌘9 connects or switches between open sessions, ⌘W goes back, and a thin key bar adds the Esc, Ctrl and arrow keys small keyboards lack.

  • Private

    No account, no tracking, no servers of its own. Passwords and your SSH key stay in this device's Keychain.

  • Safe connections

    Sign in with a password or an OpenSSH key. Host keys are pinned on first connect, and a changed key is refused.
